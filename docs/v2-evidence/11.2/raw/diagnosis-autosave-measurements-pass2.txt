D. THE §2.2 GAP, OBSERVED DIRECTLY (before the fill fix)

   One step after a resume and BEFORE any typing, on a row that held both values:
     {"full_name":"Harness Student","nrc":"","phone":"+260971234567","town":""}

   `ApplicationListSerializer` carries `full_name`, `email`, `phone` and no other
   personal answer, so hydrating from a list item wrote '' over nine stored fields.
   This is why the first pass read "NRC and phone are empty" as a wipe: the fields
   were never filled, not emptied.

E. THE MANUAL TAP IN THAT LEG (mobile project)

   Playwright reports the click as successful, the control is enabled and its box
   measures 44x44, and yet:

     native click listeners fired on the button            0
     `forceSave` called                                    no
     box before the click (viewport coords)   y = 764   [viewport height 727]
     box after  the click                     y = 297
     window.scrollY after                     1,974

   After `scrollIntoViewIfNeeded()` plus a 1.2 s settle, the same click lands:

     native click listeners fired                          1
     `forceSave` called                                   yes -> saveData(manual=true)

   The shell sets `scroll-behavior: smooth` (`src/index.css`, `src/styles/mobile.css`),
   so the auto-scroll animates and the event is dispatched at a coordinate the
   control has already left. A student taps what they can see, so this is a
   test-interaction artifact, not a product defect — but it is why the leg's
   manual-save assertion is satisfied by the 8-second autosave write rather than by
   the tap.

F. WHY THE BAR IS BELOW THE FOLD (measured, and it is a claim correction)

     [data-testid="wizard-action-bar"]  position: sticky
     rect.top 751 / rect.bottom 872     viewport height 727   document height 2,915
     nearest ancestors that break it:
       DIV.bottom-nav-content-padding animate-page-shell-in
           overflow = hidden auto / hidden / auto   transform = matrix(1,0,0,1,0,0)
       DIV.transform-gpu ... (x2)                    transform = matrix(1,0,0,1,0,0)
       MAIN.flex-1 scroll-smooth overflow-x-hidden   overflow = hidden auto
       DIV.flex min-h-screen w-full overflow-x-hidd  overflow = hidden auto
       BODY.light                                    overflow = hidden auto

   `PageShell`'s root (`src/components/ui/PageShell.tsx`) is `overflow-x-hidden`,
   and `overflow-x: hidden` computes `overflow-y: auto`, which makes it a scroll
   container. A `sticky` child of a scroll container that is as tall as its content
   has no range to stick in. So the bar computes as `sticky` and behaves as
   in-flow, at any width — not only at 375 and 390 as journey 1's comment assumed.
