== GATE-13.2-03 — every recording of F3's stale-tab reload, including the one
   that did not reproduce ==

F3 (tab/device switch) makes one assertion and takes one measurement.

The ASSERTION — "the server is the record" — passed in every recording: device 1,
reading through its own session immediately after device 2 wrote, saw device 2's
value. That is the isolation-shaped claim this bullet makes, and it is not in
question below.

The MEASUREMENT is what happens next: device 1 reloads while its wizard still
holds pre-write form state, and the file records which value the server then
holds. Auto-save is mandatory on every student form (PRODUCT.md) and `useAutoSave`
flushes a dirty form on `beforeunload`, so a stale tab reloading after another
device wrote is the one moment where a recovery cache could plausibly land on top
of a newer server value — which `.kiro/steering/enterprise-tenancy.md` forbids:

    "Local wizard snapshots are recovery caches ... never authoritative over the
     selected online draft."

THREE RECORDINGS, TWO OUTCOMES
------------------------------

  #  when            fixture stamp   device 2 wrote        server held after
                                                           device 1's reload
  -  --------------  --------------  -------------------   ------------------------
  1  during authoring  15047749      "Device two 15047749" "Lusaka"   <- REVERTED
  2  during authoring  16146525      "Device two 16146525" "Lusaka"   <- REVERTED
  3  the recorded run  16885941      "Device two 16885941" "Device two 16885941"
                                                                      <- SURVIVED

Recording 3 is the one with a preserved artifact
(`playwright-desktop.json` -> `matrix-rows.txt` line 15, plus its trace in
`artifacts-desktop/`). Recordings 1 and 2 were taken from the two earlier full
adversarial runs of this session; their JSON reports were overwritten by the runs
that followed, so only the values transcribed here survive them. They are reported
because dropping them would leave a cleaner record and a less true one.

WHAT IS THEREFORE CLAIMED
-------------------------

* The behaviour is **intermittent**: 2 reverts in 3 recordings, on byte-identical
  code, on the same box. That is the same shape 12.4 section 12.2 and 13.1 section
  6.1 established for this machine — a race whose outcome depends on timing rather
  than on input.
* The revert, when it happens, is **silent data loss across two devices** for a
  student's own draft, which is why it is filed as a finding rather than as a
  curiosity.
* The **mechanism is not confirmed**. `useAutoSave`'s `beforeunload` flush is the
  plausible cause and it was not read to prove it, no timing was instrumented, and
  nothing was changed. Whether a stale snapshot should ever win is the wizard
  lane's contract to state.

WHY THE TEST DOES NOT ASSERT IT
-------------------------------

Asserting the revert would pin a defect as correct behaviour; asserting the
survival would make a required test fail 2 runs in 3 for a reason the wizard lane
owns, not this bullet. The row asserts the claim that is stable (the server is the
record, and one draft stays one draft on both devices) and publishes the
measurement as an annotation, so the owning lane has the number either way.
