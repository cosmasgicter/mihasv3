Why the named response envelopes could not drift
-----------------------------------------------
The nine envelopes that moved into `_catalog_view_helpers.py` are built by
`apps/common/openapi_helpers.py::envelope_serializer(name, ...)` /
`paginated_serializer(name, ...)`, i.e. the component name is an **explicit
argument**, not the defining module. drf-spectacular therefore keys them on that
argument and strips the `Serializer` suffix, which is why they appear as:

    CatalogCanonicalProgramListResponse
    CatalogInstitutionListResponse
    CatalogInstitutionResponse
    CatalogIntakeListResponse
    CatalogIntakeResponse
    CatalogMessageResponse
    CatalogProgramListResponse
    CatalogProgramResponse
    CatalogSubjectListResponse
    (+ CatalogCanonicalProgramPage, CatalogProgramPage, CatalogSubjectListMeta)

A module move cannot rename any of them, and the byte-identical diff above is the
measurement rather than the argument.
