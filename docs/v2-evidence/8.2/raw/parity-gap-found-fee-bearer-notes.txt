--- Diagnosis ----------------------------------------------------------------

The legacy mutator ``_update_payment_status`` persisted four provider fields
that ``_transition`` did not: ``fee``, ``bearer``, and (on failure) ``notes``
from ``reasonForFailure``. The hardened webhook branch worked around the notes
gap with its own out-of-band ``payment.save(update_fields=["notes", ...])``
before calling ``_transition`` — so the webhook path had notes and the verify
path did not, and ``fee``/``bearer`` were lost on both.

Fix (one place, the sole writer — ADR-007):
``PaymentCoreMixin._transition`` now captures ``fee`` (via ``_parse_amount``),
``bearer``, and ``notes`` (only for ``target_status == "failed"``, truncated to
500 chars) from ``provider_data``, matching the legacy conditions exactly. The
duplicate out-of-band notes write in ``apply_webhook_event`` was removed.
